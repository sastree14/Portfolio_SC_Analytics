# Business impact

## What changes

1. Forecast quality is read by decision horizon rather than as one blended score.
2. Bias, stock coverage and service risk are reviewed together.
3. Replenishment decisions can be traced back to the demand signal and forecast evidence.
4. Product and market drill-downs use one semantic definition layer.

## Example operating scenario

A planner reviews WAPE and bias at 1, 3, 6 and 9 months, checks stock coverage and service level, then decides whether to maintain, accelerate or defer a purchase plan.

## KPIs

- WAPE
- forecast bias
- stock coverage
- service level
- forecast horizon
- recommended replenishment action

These are public-example KPIs. The repository does not claim measured client impact.
