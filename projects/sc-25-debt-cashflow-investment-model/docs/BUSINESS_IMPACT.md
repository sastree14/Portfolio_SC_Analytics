# Business impact

1. Connect financing structure to project cash flows
2. Measure covenant headroom under multiple scenarios
3. Separate operating performance from financing effects
4. Calculate return and debt-service metrics consistently

## Example

An investment has construction drawdowns, amortizing debt and operating cash flows. The model projects debt service, covenant headroom and investor returns across scenarios.

## KPIs

- DSCR
- IRR
- NPV
- debt balance
- covenant headroom
