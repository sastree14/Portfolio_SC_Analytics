# SC-25 · Debt, Cash Flow & Investment Model

**An integrated debt and investment model for repayment schedules, covenant headroom, returns and downside analysis.**

Designed for **Infrastructure · real estate · corporate finance · project finance · investment review**.

## Business impact

- Connect financing structure to project cash flows
- Measure covenant headroom under multiple scenarios
- Separate operating performance from financing effects
- Calculate return and debt-service metrics consistently

## Example use case

An investment has construction drawdowns, amortizing debt and operating cash flows. The model projects debt service, covenant headroom and investor returns across scenarios.

## Technology at a glance

**Python · Pandas · NumPy · OpenPyXL · Plotly · XIRR · PostgreSQL · Excel**
