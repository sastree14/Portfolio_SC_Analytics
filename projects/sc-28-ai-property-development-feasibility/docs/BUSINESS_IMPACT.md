# Business impact

1. Bring acquisition assumptions, development costs and financing into one feasibility view
2. Use structured document extraction to reduce manual review
3. Compare downside and upside cases before committing capital
4. Track approval and due-diligence steps through one workflow

## Example

A potential development site is loaded with acquisition cost, build assumptions and financing terms. Documents are summarized, scenarios are calculated and the project moves through defined review gates.

## KPIs

- project IRR
- development margin
- cost variance
- approval cycle time
- scenario downside
