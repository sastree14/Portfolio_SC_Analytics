# Business impact

## What changes

1. Service level and order exceptions are reviewed in one operating interface.
2. Lead-time performance can be segmented by customer, destination and Incoterm.
3. Managers can separate systemic delays from isolated data or commitment issues.
4. The same semantic definitions support executive and operational drill-down.

## Example operating scenario

An operations lead reviews service level, checks late-delivery exceptions, drills into the affected destination or customer and prioritizes the orders requiring intervention.

## KPIs

- service level
- on-time orders
- average lead time
- open exceptions
- overdue orders
- commitment adherence

These are public-example KPIs. The repository does not claim measured client impact.
