# Business impact

1. Measure portfolio risk using more than standalone asset volatility
2. Compare allocation choices under explicit constraints
3. Stress the portfolio under adverse return scenarios
4. Translate risk appetite into a reproducible allocation problem

## Example

A portfolio must satisfy asset-class limits and a maximum risk budget. The engine estimates covariance, runs stress scenarios and produces feasible allocations for different risk targets.

## KPIs

- expected return
- volatility
- VaR
- CVaR
- Sharpe ratio
