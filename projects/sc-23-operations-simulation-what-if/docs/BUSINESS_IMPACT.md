# Business impact

1. Test operational changes without disrupting live processes
2. Quantify waiting time, utilization and bottleneck risk
3. Compare staffing and demand scenarios under uncertainty
4. Separate average outcomes from tail-risk behaviour

## Example

A service operation wants to know whether one additional specialist is enough for a projected 20% demand increase. The simulator compares waiting time and utilization distributions under both scenarios.

## KPIs

- average wait
- P95 wait
- resource utilization
- throughput
- queue length
