# SC-29 · Commercial Due Diligence & Market Intelligence System

**A structured research system for market sizing, competitor evidence, company signals and investment-question tracking.**

Designed for **Private equity · corporate development · strategy · investment research · consulting**.

## Business impact

- Turn an open-ended research process into explicit evidence questions
- Separate source evidence from analyst interpretation
- Combine market, company and financial signals in one review structure
- Make assumptions and confidence levels visible

## Example use case

An investment team defines ten commercial questions. The system collects approved public evidence, stores source-level records, summarizes only from retrieved material and flags unanswered or weakly supported questions.

## Technology at a glance

**Python · Pandas · DuckDB · FastAPI · Playwright · BeautifulSoup · OpenAI · PostgreSQL · Plotly**
