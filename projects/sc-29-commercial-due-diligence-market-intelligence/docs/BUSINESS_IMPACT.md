# Business impact

1. Turn an open-ended research process into explicit evidence questions
2. Separate source evidence from analyst interpretation
3. Combine market, company and financial signals in one review structure
4. Make assumptions and confidence levels visible

## Example

An investment team defines ten commercial questions. The system collects approved public evidence, stores source-level records, summarizes only from retrieved material and flags unanswered or weakly supported questions.

## KPIs

- questions answered
- evidence items per claim
- source recency
- review exceptions
- confidence coverage
