# Business impact

## What changes

1. Move beyond a probability-of-default score to a complete lending decision
2. Balance expected revenue against expected loss
3. Make approval thresholds and risk appetite explicit
4. Explain important risk drivers for each application

## Example operating scenario

For each application the engine estimates default risk, expected loss and expected contribution, then recommends approve, decline or review under a defined risk appetite.

## KPIs

- ROC AUC
- PR AUC
- expected loss
- approval rate
- risk-adjusted contribution

These are measurable project KPIs. The repository does not claim organization-wide production impact where it has not been measured.
