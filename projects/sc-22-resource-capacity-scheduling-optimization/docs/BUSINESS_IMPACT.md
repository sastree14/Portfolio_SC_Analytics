# Business impact

1. Allocate constrained resources consistently
2. Expose capacity bottlenecks before they become delays
3. Balance utilization against service-level commitments
4. Compare feasible schedules instead of relying on manual planning

## Example

A service team has jobs with deadlines, required skills and estimated durations. The engine assigns work to available people while minimizing lateness and excessive utilization.

## KPIs

- utilization
- unassigned work
- late jobs
- overtime hours
- solver objective
