# Business impact

## What changes

1. Allocate limited inventory where it creates the most value
2. Respect capacity, stock and service constraints explicitly
3. Compare optimized decisions against current or heuristic policies
4. Turn forecasts into operational actions

## Example operating scenario

Available inventory must be allocated across locations with different demand, margin and service targets. The optimizer produces the highest-value feasible allocation and reports the trade-offs.

## KPIs

- total cost
- service level
- stockout units
- inventory utilization
- solver gap

These are measurable project KPIs. The repository does not claim organization-wide production impact where it has not been measured.
