# Business impact

1. Quantify the trade-off between price, demand and contribution margin
2. Compare pricing scenarios before rollout
3. Respect floor prices, margin targets and commercial constraints
4. Turn historical response into a repeatable pricing decision process

## Example

A product has declining conversion and a margin floor. The engine estimates response to price changes and chooses the highest expected contribution within commercial constraints.

## KPIs

- contribution margin
- expected demand
- price elasticity
- revenue
- constraint violations
