# Business impact

1. Make financial assumptions explicit and editable
2. Connect operational drivers to P&L and cash flow
3. Compare base, downside and upside scenarios consistently
4. Identify the variables that matter most to cash runway and profitability

## Example

Management adjusts volume growth, price, gross margin and hiring assumptions. The engine recalculates monthly P&L and cash runway across base, upside and downside scenarios.

## KPIs

- revenue
- EBITDA
- cash balance
- runway
- free cash flow
