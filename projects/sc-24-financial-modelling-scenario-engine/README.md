# SC-24 · Financial Modelling & Scenario Engine

**A driver-based financial model that links revenue, margin, operating costs and cash flow to explicit business assumptions.**

Designed for **CFO teams · startups · investment analysis · business planning · scenario review**.

## Business impact

- Make financial assumptions explicit and editable
- Connect operational drivers to P&L and cash flow
- Compare base, downside and upside scenarios consistently
- Identify the variables that matter most to cash runway and profitability

## Example use case

Management adjusts volume growth, price, gross margin and hiring assumptions. The engine recalculates monthly P&L and cash runway across base, upside and downside scenarios.

## Technology at a glance

**Python · Pandas · NumPy · OpenPyXL · Plotly · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Excel**
